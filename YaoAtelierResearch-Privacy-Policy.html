01 / OVERVIEW

About Yao Atelier Research

Yao Atelier Research is the name of this research project and its planned internal application. Yao Atelier is the public-facing jewelry brand under which the individual developer operates the project. Yao Atelier is a handmade jewelry brand serving customers in the United States. The application’s intended purpose is to understand where relevant jewelry Pins lead, including marketplace listings and independent stores. The application is developed and operated by an individual using Yao Atelier as the public-facing brand. The individual operator controls the application and is responsible for its data handling. Yao Atelier and Yao Atelier Research are brand and project names, not separate legal entities designated as the operator. The same individual developer is responsible for this project and can be contacted at the privacy email below.

The application is not currently operating a Pinterest API connection. This policy describes current feasibility work and a planned research service. The Pinterest integration and the complete privacy-request service are not yet operational. No Pinterest API research data has been collected for this planned application. It does not claim that Pinterest has approved, endorsed, or partnered with Yao Atelier or its application. Publishing this policy would not itself authorize API access or research.

This policy covers this research project, not purchases made through Etsy or other stores. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Earlier feasibility research. Before the planned API integration, web research tools were used to query public search indexes. Local working files retain 15 unverified Pin leads, including search terms, public Pin URLs and identifiers, index-supplied titles, and supporting search-result and research notes. These are not Pinterest API responses or verified destination links. No Pinterest account was connected for that research. Search queries and returned public results were also processed in the external research-tool environment.

02 / YOUR AUTHORIZATION

Connecting through Pinterest OAuth

If the application receives the necessary permissions and a connection is enabled, an authorized account holder will be directed to Pinterest to sign in and review the permissions requested by the application. Authorization is optional. We will not ask you to give us your Pinterest password or session cookies.

After authorization, Pinterest returns an authorization code that the application exchanges for an access token and, where applicable, a refresh token. These credentials allow permitted requests on the account holder’s behalf. They are sensitive credentials, not research results.

The proposed connection would request boards:read and pins:read, subject to Pinterest’s endpoint and account-access restrictions. These scopes do not grant unrestricted access to all public Pins. We do not plan to request permission to publish, edit, delete, message, or read secret boards. Any material expansion would require a policy review and any additional authorization required.

Your OAuth consent does not grant access to unrelated accounts or substitute for Pinterest’s approval of restricted features or research uses. See Pinterest’s authorization documentation.

03 / DATA SCOPE

Information we may access

Only information necessary for an authorized research task will be used. Depending on the endpoint, permissions, and response, this may include:

Connection information
Authorization credentials, granted scopes, expiry information, and an account identifier if needed to associate a connection with its authorized holder.
Board and Pin information
Board identifiers and names; Pin identifiers, titles, descriptions, destination links, and relevant creation or retrieval dates. Responses may also contain owner identifiers or media references; unnecessary personal or media fields will not be retained.
Research records
Search terms, Pin reference URLs, verified outbound links, destination domains, product categories, marketplace classifications, verification status, and minimal evidence needed to audit a permitted finding.
Privacy correspondence
Contact details and information you choose to provide when emailing lvnhk.trade@gmail.com with a privacy question or deletion request.
Existing feasibility files
Public search-index leads and supporting evidence retained from the earlier feasibility work described above. Titles and search-result excerpts can contain names or other publicly available personal information; public availability does not remove privacy considerations.

We do not plan to request private messages, payment details, customer orders, or secret-board content. This project does not require browsing histories or individual consumer profiles. Public information can still be personal information and will be handled accordingly.

04 / PURPOSE & PERMISSIONS

A limited research purpose

The intended research examines handmade jewelry, pearl bracelets, something blue jewelry, evil eye bracelets, and bridesmaid jewelry. The initial pilot would target no more than 20 verified Pins. It aims to verify outbound destinations and distinguish Etsy, eBay, Poshmark, Amazon, and independent stores, including stores using Shopify or WooCommerce. A store’s technology will not be inferred from its domain alone.

Any third-party keyword search through the Pinterest API would depend on access to an appropriate approved feature, such as the limited-availability partner search endpoint. Ordinary account-read permissions are not a general competitor-search license.

Third-party Pinterest data, including competitor content, will only be accessed when the necessary platform permissions, account authorizations where required, and research-use permissions are in place. Required written approval must cover any restricted search feature, competitor research, benchmarking, cross-account combination, retention, or disclosure involved. If those permissions are unavailable, that activity will not proceed.

The application will not scrape Pinterest or bypass login requirements, CAPTCHAs, or other access restrictions. We do not intend to sell Pinterest data, use it for advertising audiences or individual profiling, or use it to train or improve AI or machine-learning models. Small research samples will not be presented as Pinterest-wide market share.

05 / DATA LIFECYCLE

Storage, retention, and deletion

Pinterest’s applicable terms and any specific written permissions govern whether data may be stored at all. This policy does not create a right to retain API responses or export them to CSV. Without permission to store a category of Pinterest data, the application will not persist that data.

Planned local storage. If permitted storage is enabled, the initial pilot’s research records will be held on the individual developer’s Mac, separate from the website repository. Privacy email is handled through Gmail and is not confined to that local storage. GitHub Pages will not host research records or authorization credentials.

Existing local files and tooling. Earlier feasibility CSV files, reports, and search evidence remain on the developer’s Mac, outside the dedicated pilot database. They are retained to document the feasibility assessment and its limitations. Their retention schedule has not been finalized; deletion requires manual review of these files and any copies under the operator’s control. The local cleanup utility does not manage these historical files, Gmail, external research-tool service records, credentials, or backups. Its pilot database currently contains no research records; local tests have used synthetic records and request metadata. Tool settings are not a verified end-to-end retention or deletion guarantee.

Retention arrangements are not yet finalized. This policy does not promise an automatic deletion service or a fixed retention or deletion period that has not been verified. Before Pinterest API research data is collected, the operator will establish and verify a retention schedule based on the permitted research purpose, applicable Pinterest permissions, and any legal requirements, and update this policy accordingly. Pinterest API collection will not begin while those arrangements remain unresolved.

Deletion requests. Please email lvnhk.trade@gmail.com to ask about or request deletion of information under the operator’s control. Requests require manual review and, where needed, proportionate verification of authority. Applicable legal and platform deadlines take precedence; this policy does not defer those obligations. No automated mailbox monitoring, identity verification, or end-to-end deletion capability is claimed.

The planned deletion process must account for local records, exports, relevant logs, credentials, and backup copies under the operator’s control. These arrangements, including handling of device downtime, have not been fully verified. Revoking access through Pinterest does not itself delete copies already held. The operator must stop access that is no longer authorized and address retained copies under the applicable requirements.

Privacy correspondence may include your email address and the information needed to handle your inquiry or request. It will be used for that purpose and any applicable legal obligation. The detailed correspondence retention schedule remains to be finalized. Please do not email passwords, API tokens, identity documents, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Credential storage, backup handling, and device encryption have not been verified for a live research service. This policy does not claim automatic erasure of credentials, backups, or device snapshots. Research data, credentials, and privacy correspondence will not be included in the public website repository.

06 / ACCESS & SAFEGUARDS

How information would be protected

Access will be limited to authorized people working on the approved research for Yao Atelier. Any service provider handling research data would need to be necessary for that purpose, subject to appropriate safeguards, and permitted by Pinterest’s terms and applicable authorizations. No third-party Pinterest data provider has been selected for this application.

Before enabling a connection, we will put in place protected credential storage, encrypted transport, restricted access to retained files, and a working deletion process. Credentials will not appear in public HTML, browser-accessible code, research reports, or public logs. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Internal reports, aggregate results, or any external disclosure will be produced only to the extent permitted. Aggregating information does not automatically remove Pinterest’s restrictions. We may disclose limited information where legally required; this does not authorize unrelated commercial sharing.
